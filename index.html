Privacy Policy for Gmail OTP Fetcher

Effective Date: [Insert Date]

This Privacy Policy explains how the Chrome Extension Gmail OTP Fetcher (“Extension”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our browser extension available through the Chrome Web Store.

1. Information We Collect

The Extension may access and process the following information:

  • Your Gmail email address
  • The content of Gmail messages that match specific criteria (e.g., OTP-related emails)

We use Google OAuth 2.0 to authenticate your Gmail account and only request the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly scope. No write access is ever requested or used.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your Gmail data is used solely to:

  • Identify the latest OTP sent to your inbox
  • Autofill the OTP field during verification on websites like Ticketmaster

All data is processed locally in your browser. No personal information or email content is transmitted to any server or stored externally.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share your data with any third parties. The extension operates completely within your browser and does not transmit data outside of your device.

4. User Consent

We use Google’s OAuth flow to obtain your explicit consent before accessing your Gmail. You may revoke access at any time via:

  • Your Google Account security settings
  • Removing the extension from Chrome

5. Your Choices

  • You may uninstall the extension at any time
  • You may revoke Gmail access at any time

6. Security

We use OAuth 2.0 for secure access. No credentials or sensitive data are stored or transmitted. Data access is limited strictly to reading OTP-related emails.
